Lieblingsbilder · Restore from Google Drive

Turns the sealed files of your "Lieblingsbilder" folder in Google Drive back into your pictures, on this computer alone. Nothing is sent anywhere: this page talks to no server and works offline. You need the folder (downloaded from Drive, as a zip or unpacked) and your encryption password.

1. The files from Drive

In Google Drive, right-click the folder "Lieblingsbilder" and choose Download. Drive gives you one or more zip files; choose them all here (or the unpacked folder). Its subfolder "Archive (encrypted)" holds the key file lieblingsbilder-keys.lb and one .lb file per picture; readable copies, if you keep any, are left alone.

2. Your encryption password

The one you chose when you turned on encrypted sync in the app. It is used only here, in this browser, to open the key file.

3. What is in the backup

4. Restore

Pictures are written as they were, named by the date taken and their original name, one folder per folder of the app. Photos of a protected folder are included once it is opened above.
